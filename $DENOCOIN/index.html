THE DENO DEVVING GUIDE

Less figuring it out.
More getting started.

A walkthrough of your first account, your first signal, and the tools you’ll use along the way.

YOUR FIRST VISIT

Make yourself at home.

Open Deno Devving and choose Create account. Add your name, username, email, and a password of at least 12 characters. Confirm it to create your account and Solana wallet.

Download the encrypted wallet backup when prompted, then open your workspace.

Create your account ↗
YOUR SIGNAL DESK

Give the feed your attention.

Start in All Feeds. Switch between posts and follows, use the search field to find a topic, and open Filters to narrow the view.

Use Pause feed when you need time to read. Open the tracker controls to manage the accounts you follow.

FROM IDEA TO TOKEN

Give your idea a name.

  1. Sign in and unlock your wallet.
  2. Open Create or Creation Panel.
  3. Enter the token name, symbol, description, and image.
  4. Review the selected launchpad, network, fees, and transaction details before confirming.

Your recorded launches appear in Your Deploys. Keep SOL available for network and launchpad fees.

Open the creation panel ↗
✧

Review before you launch.

Token creation is an on-chain action. Check the details and fee estimate in the app before signing.

YOUR NEXT MOVE

Keep an eye on your tokens.

Select a token from Your Deploys to open its trading tools, or use the wallet’s Buy / Sell tokens option to look up a token by mint address.

Choose buy or sell, enter an amount, and review the quote, slippage, and fees. Use the wallet menu to receive or withdraw SOL.

Confirm the destination address and network before making a transfer.

⇄

Every transaction deserves a check.

Quotes and execution depend on liquidity, network conditions, and your settings. Review each transaction in the app.

KEEP YOUR WALLET YOURS

A little care goes a long way.

Save the encrypted JSON file offered during account setup. You can also download an Encrypted backup from the wallet menu.

To import a saved file, choose Create account → Already have an encrypted wallet backup? and select your JSON file. Enter the password that protects that file.

Keep the file and its password somewhere safe. A password-protected JSON file requires its encryption password to unlock.

◇

Private means private.

Never post your private key, backup file, or wallet password in chat, social media, or a support conversation.

Talk to the team ↗
How are Deno Coin and Deno Devving connected?

They share the Deno brand and community. Deno Coin is the community token, while Deno Devving is a Solana workspace for feeds, token creation, and wallet tools.

How do I start using Deno Devving?

Open the app, choose Create account, and complete signup. Save your encrypted wallet backup before entering your workspace. The guide above walks through the main tools.

Do I need SOL to use the wallet tools?

On-chain transactions require SOL for network fees. Token creation and trading can also include platform or launchpad fees. Review the fees and network shown in the app before confirming.

Can I restore my wallet from a backup?

A Deno Devving encrypted backup can restore the same wallet address when you provide the password that protects that file. Keep your backup and password safe.

Where can I find the Deno Coin community?

Visit @denocoin78 on X, or find the official Instagram and TikTok links in the footer.
