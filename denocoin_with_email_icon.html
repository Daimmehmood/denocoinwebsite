How to Buy$DENO

Follow these simple steps to join the prehistoric pack

1

Get a Wallet

Download Phantom or Solflare wallet from the App Store or Google Play Store for free. Desktop users, get the browser extension.

2

Get Some SOL

Have SOL in your wallet to swap for $DENO. Buy SOL directly through your wallet, transfer from another wallet, or buy on an exchange and send it to your wallet.

3

Go to Raydium

Connect to Raydium. Go to raydium.io in your browser or app. Connect your wallet. Paste the $DENO token address into Raydium and confirm.

4

Swap SOL for $DENO

Swap your SOL for $DENO. We have ZERO taxes so you don't need to worry about specific slippage, although you may need to use slippage during times of market volatility.
